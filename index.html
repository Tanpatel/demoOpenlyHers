BUILT WITH CARE

Support should feel safe.

OpenlyHer is peer support, not therapy or emergency care. We plan to make crisis resources visible immediately when a message suggests danger, with human review alongside safety tools. Our approach will be reviewed by a licensed mental health professional before launch.
